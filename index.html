About

A Quick Intro

My journey in IT began back in my high-school years. In the healthcare sector I laid the groundwork for the systems and infrastructure side through critical infrastructure builds (Citrix, VLAN, golden image) and on-site HBYS management. I joined Etiya in 2021 as System Support Specialist, became Senior in 2023 and Principal Specialist in 2025. Today I'm responsible for the smooth running of identity management, email systems, server operations, backup and disaster recovery across the company.

In my day-to-day work I manage the Microsoft Entra ID, SCCM and VMware vSphere environments, and automate repetitive processes with n8n and Jira to ease the team's load. With the "Architecting on AWS" certification I earned in 2025, I'm focusing more on the cloud and hybrid infrastructure side. I believe a technical solution only lasts when it's backed by good documentation, clear corporate language and strong teamwork.
