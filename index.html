About

A Quick Intro

I got into IT in high school, graduating from the Information Technologies track of a vocational high school. My first job was in the same field: as IT Manager at Apex Surgical Medical Center I built a surgical center's infrastructure from the ground up — a Citrix environment on an HP server, a network segmented with VLANs, and golden-image endpoint provisioning standardized with WDS. I then became the on-site lead for Akgün Yazılım at a public hospital, looking after the HIS (hospital information system) doctors and nurses use all day. Today I work at Etiya as Principal Specialist — System & Infrastructure.

I joined Etiya in 2021 as System Support Specialist; over time I became Senior Specialist (2023) and then Principal Specialist (2025). Today I'm responsible for keeping the accounts, email systems and servers that everyone in the company relies on running smoothly. As a team we look after security, user management, backups, disaster recovery and process automation.

On the technical side I mostly work with Microsoft Entra ID, SCCM and VMware vSphere; bringing on-prem Active Directory and Entra ID together under a single identity and automating repetitive tasks with n8n + Jira is part of my daily routine. With the "Architecting on AWS" certification I earned in 2025, I'm spending more time on the cloud side. The most important thing I learned in the field: a solution works when it's explained as well as it's built. So in documentation, reporting and corporate communication I take care to write complex infrastructure in language everyone can understand. The technical part matters, but so does building things together with the team.
