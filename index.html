About

A Quick Intro

I started my career in private-sector technical support; with Akgün Bilgisayar I was assigned on-site to a public health project at Çorum Chest Diseases Hospital, and today I continue at Etiya as Principal Specialist — System and Infrastructure Management.

I joined Etiya in 2021 as System Support Specialist; over time I was promoted to Senior Specialist (2023) and then Principal Specialist (2025). In my current role I'm responsible for keeping the user accounts, email systems and server infrastructure used by employees across the company running without interruption. Together with my team we cover system security, user management, backups, disaster recovery and process automation.

My technical focus is Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), SCCM and VMware vSphere; alongside these I work on hybrid identity (on-prem Active Directory ↔ Entra ID) and process automation (n8n + Jira). With the "Architecting on AWS" certification I earned in 2025, I'm investing further in the cloud side. With the experience I've gained on the field I take a solution-oriented and adaptive approach, and as a professional who values teamwork I aim to create both technical and cultural value.
